[ v0.4.1 ]
New Features:
- Rin can now sit on chairs/benches. Right click on a chair and choose Sit.

- Added a new Equipment UI for Rin. Right click on her and choose Equipment. You can customize the UI size in settings.
- Rin can now loot containers by right click > Rin: Loot.
- New jobs: Loot Area & Clear Plants (removes bushes/grass).
- New tabs in the home menu: Menu Order (lets you re-order the right click menu), Loot Settings, Bug Report.
- New settings: show relationship change popups (disabled by default) and sit down when you sit (enabled by default).
- New kill counter system (only increases when Rin kills zombies).
- Added a new What's New UI.
- There is now a separate bar for Relationship/Romance in her info tab.
- Improved Rin's pathfinding while performing jobs.
- Added more unique dialogue-line reactions to receiving loot.
- Rin will now store her weapon on her back instead of in her inventory.
- Rin will react if you swing a melee weapon at her while out of combat.
- Rin will now sit when you do.

Compatibility:
- Mutants: Fixed Mutants turning Rin into a zombie in SP.
- Dinosaur/Wolves Mods: Rin will now target wolves/dinosaurs if those mods are enabled.
- TwisTonFire - Proximity Inventory: Rin's loot is now hidden from proximity loot.
- BetterContainers/RPG Inventory/etc.: Smart loot will no longer deposit into her inventory.

Bug Fixes:
- Rin will now loot in whole stacks instead of individual items in MP/SP.
- Increased Rin's inventory storage (from 30000 characters to 60000) to prevent item despawning.
- Added safeguards to prevent items being removed while getting in and out of vehicles.
- Rin's stats now persist on dedicated MP servers when relogging.
- Rin's spawning is now atomic: her body is claimed/marked before everything else is rendered.
- Fixed LUA errors from Maintenance gains caused by meleeing with a non-melee weapon.
- Fixed flashlight darkness false-positive calls.
- Flashlight no longer switches on in a lit interior during the day.

- Rin may struggle to loot containers in corners.
- Rin's pathing may be weird at times when sent to perform jobs.

Bug Reports:
- The new procedure for reporting issues is to open the Bug Reports menu, turn on Detailed Logging, record the issue, click Generate Report, paste that into pastebin.com, open the workshop page, and then paste that pastebin report link plus your console log (on another pastebin) into it.
- Do not just post "X is broken" on the workshop page. Please provide the reports so we can debug the issues quickly & efficiently.

More dialogue lines have been added as well.

- Rin can now pry open doors with a crowbar (the failure rate decreases with strength).
- Rin can now take showers with the [B42:SP/MP] Take A Bath And Shower mod enabled.
- Rin can now exercise once a day, either by right-clicking or through the settings menu. Rin can exercise with you if you enable a setting in the settings menu.

- Rin can now lie on beds by right-clicking, the same as chairs.
- Added a new setting to adjust Rin's sensitivity to your aim cone with guns. You can also disable it.
- Improved the settings menu display (done by Albrecht).
- 50+ new idle and chatter triggers.

- Rin no longer triggers A-Life NPC companions' fear (needs verification).
- Rin should not be targeted by Division Gadgets mod.
- You now get a warning if you try to start the game with zombies disabled, since that prevents the NPC from spawning.

Bug Fixes:

- Fixed the flashlight triggering indoors when it is not dark.
- Fixed food being restored in full on inventory transfers.
- Fixed an MP server issue where deleting a torch battery caused anti-cheat triggers.
- Added SP fast-forward support to most jobs, including sitting.
- Fixed fast forward causing Rin to suffer a tragic chair death.

[ v0.5 ]
Locked and Loaded

New Features:
- Rin can now equip and use firearms. Right-click on any firearm and choose Equip primary firearm or equip one in the equipment menu.
- New Settings: 5 new settings are now under a Gun menu inside the Fight menu:
  - Gun use (turns guns on/off)
  - Melee switch (makes her switch to melee weapons when zombies are close)
  - Reload between fights (she'll swap mags between fights)
  - Magazine care (she fills magazines while idle)
  - Ammo reserve (when she chooses to use guns).

- Ammo Breakdown: Rin will automatically break down ammunition from cartons into ammo boxes and loose ammunition to reload her weapons.
- Home system: enabling auto-use home ammunition will allow her to top off her own magazines while at home. She will only take what she needs and place back ammo boxes from cartons into the container she took it from.
- New Skills: Added Aiming/Reloading.

- Guns: Tested with vanilla, Guns of 93, Brita, and Marz gun mods.

Bug Fixes:
- Optimizations: Made 15+ fixes to improve the mod's performance across menus, inventory transfers, jobs, etc. Unified 50 separate zombie scans into 2, which should lead to better performance across the board.
- Fixed a Lua error while building.

Future Planned Features (not in this patch):
- Being able to equip a primary/secondary firearm.
- Adding support for gun attachments (laser sights, silencers, etc).
- Adding support for target highlighting used in some aiming mods.
- Adding support for different fire modes (single, burst, full-auto).

Known Issues:
- Not all gun mods may work or there might still be some incompatibility issues. She may not recognize the ammunition, magazines, etc. Post on the workshop page which mods have incompatibility issues.

[ v0.6 ]
First Contact

New Additions:

Pre-Ingame Menu & How You Meet Them:
- You can now customize your companion before loading into the game.
- You can now choose how you meet them: spawn together with you, close by, or in a random town.
- You can customize the distance they spawn from you or even pick a random town.
- Current starting scenarios: hold-up (inside a house), vehicle (spawns near a car on the road), and scavenger (find her out in the open). Note: more scenarios are planned for the future.

Radial Menu:
- Added a quick access radial menu. Hold Mouse 4 (the key can be changed in the Sandbox settings) to open it.
- Console users use the left stick.

Home Deposit System:
- Rin can now deposit loot into containers with right click > Deposit Loot, via the work menu, or through a new home system.
- Mark a grid as home, then right click a container and mark it as storage, then tell her to store loot with right click > Work > Loot > Deposit Loot at Home, or by choosing it from the radial menu.
- When told to deposit all loot, Rin will smartly deposit into containers that already hold similar items.
- You can customize the storage settings in the Loot & Storage menu inside her home menu.

Changes:
- Updated melee combat footwork: she can now backpedal while swinging weapons, prefers to fight on the flanks, and will flee when surrounded.
- Updated gun combat footwork: she now backpedals and keeps her distance from zombies, can shove when they get close, will run if surrounded, and will flee to reload her magazines when the melee switch is off until she has reloaded, then resume combat.
- Rebalanced gunplay: reduced her visible aiming cone, added a longer target acquisition timer, and added a brief timer between targets to stop her from snapping.
- Melee and gun target acquisition now prioritizes Sprinters > Fast Shamblers > Shamblers > Crawlers.
- Rin can now move backwards and will step out of your way more cleanly when you bump into her.
- Sheet Rope/Ladders: Rin now teleports to follow you when you move up and down ladders or sheet ropes.
- Home System: now works on multiple floors (you have to enable it with a check mark).
- Rin now says goodnight and good morning when you sleep and gives you a stat reduction depending on relationship state.
- Rin can now eject her own magazines on command; there is a 15 second cooldown before she resumes ammo and magazine care. (Shotgun/revolver ammo ejection will come next).
- Guns of Marz: all rifles now hit a minimum of 2 targets instead of 1.
- Added 20 new combat callouts.
- Rin now makes callouts when the helicopter is out.
- Added missing /RU/IT/ES/DE translations and Portuguese (new).
- Updated the Sleep System to be more compatible with other sleep mods (might still not work there's 10+ sleep mods).

Bug Fixes:
- Fixed Rin forgetting her primary gun on SP when spawning.
- Fixed Rin not snapping to you when you are on floors above or below her.
- Guns in holsters now unequip when removed.
- Fixed a LUA error from the gift tooltip probing non-item tooltips.
- Fixed a LUA error with Improved Flashlights.
- Horde detection now requires visible targets only.

Optimizations:
- Overhauled the ammo system to cache inventory information and reduce inventory scans.
- Overhauled the melee and gun systems to share target data and reduce zombie scans.
- Unified the light detection system and added caching to reduce repeated light calls.
- Item transfer stack sizes are now weight dependent like vanilla (lighter items use more stacks, heavier items use fewer) instead of a fixed 20.
- Optimized inventory saving for large inventories to improve performance.
- Fixed excessive paperdoll refreshing on the Info menu tab.

Compatibility:
- Guns of Tien, B42 Real Firearms, Gunsmith 1993: added full support for ammunition, magazines, and guns.
- NPC Vision: added compatibility.
- Spiked Barrels: Rin is now immune to the traps.
- Auto All: Rin is no longer recognized as a zombie.
- Girlfriend Mod: Rin no longer beats them up out of jealousy.
- Buttstroke / Gun Stock Attack: added soft support to replace gun shove.

[ Experimental Build ]
Bug Fixes:
- Restored the global Bridge table during game-load callbacks when it is nil, with a one-shot restore log. The skills UI now uses the same recovery path before installing its wrappers.
- Picnic tables no longer count as beds. Sitting uses the seating path, and lying or bed planning no longer accepts them.
- Parked car callouts no longer fire while the car is stationary, reversing, or in neutral. Car-ahead, drive-state, and tired-at-night lines now require forward motion.
- Fixed the pregame controller stack overflow by excluding clothing controls from the screen-level joypad generator. The screen resolves the joypad before generating rows and rebuilds them once when focus arrives late.

Changes:
- Added the Darkness Impairment sandbox setting. Darkness now scales her melee and gun acquisition and retention range, with Off, Mild, Realistic, and Harsh levels. A flashlight raises the factor. Added the sight status and selftest command.
- Renamed the speech font setting to Speech Text Size. The presets are Normal (default), Large, and Huge. Speech Duration is now 5.25, 7, or 9 seconds. Overhead and in-car speech use the selected size and duration, and routine lines wait for the current line to finish.
- Added tooltips for Darkness Impairment, Speech Text Size, and Speech Duration in the sandbox options.
- Synced the Italian translation from the translator delivery. Removed 38 obsolete keys, adopted the translator wording, and kept the seven defective entries corrected. Preserved the 16 newer sandbox keys for Darkness Impairment, Speech Text Size, and Speech Duration because the delivery predates them.
- Added a Knox Survivors compatibility shim. It keeps Rin out of Knox Survivors threat, awareness, retreat, and event targeting without editing Knox Survivors. The shim auto-detects the mod, is inert when it is absent, and can be removed at runtime with BridgeKnoxSurvivors.uninstall().

Optimizations:
- Skill level lookups now reuse per-perk XP thresholds and a computed level. Repeated Aiming level queries no longer walk the full level table on every update.
- XP multiplier and cap lookups no longer allocate a closure per call.
- XP popups now compute font height, row height, and zoom once per frame instead of once per popup.

Compatibility:
- Knox Survivors (Workshop 3749727604): added a client-side threat guard. Single-player only; multiplayer is unverified.
- Italian translation updated.

Notes:
- The 16 newer Italian sandbox entries remain in English until the translator supplies them.
