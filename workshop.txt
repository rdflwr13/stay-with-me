description=[list]
description=[*]Shows up next to you when you enter the world.
description=[*]Follows you and runs when you run, sneaks when you sneak. You choose whether she keeps closer or farther.
description=[*]Opens doors by hand and closes them behind her, climbs fences and windows on the move, gets over tall fences.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/WmMETVJ.png[/img]
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/L4wdCaw.gif[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Fights zombies near you with whatever they hold. You assign the weapon.
description=[*]You tell them how to fight: [b]Cover me[/b], [b]Clear around us[/b], [b]Go after them[/b], [b]Back me up[/b], [b]Stay behind me[/b].
description=[*]Can't be killed: not by zombies, players, firearms, cars or fire.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/mDdNsHf.png[/img]
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/dD79K4E.gif[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Treats your wounds on her own or when you ask: disinfects, stitches, pulls out glass and bullets, splints, bandages.
description=[*]If you catch fire, she puts you out with water or an extinguisher and treats the burn.
description=[*]Notices a bite, glass in a wound, bleeding, pain, panic or hunger and says it when it matters. In a fight she keeps it short.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/1yVGiN4.png[/img]
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/72r3keF.gif[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Talks to you without dialogue windows: chat, joke, compliment, comfort, flirt, hug.
description=[*]Her answer depends on the moment and on how close you are. Your relationship grows slowly, day by day: you see it in how she behaves, not in numbers.
description=[*]Lifts your mood: having her around and talking to her eases sadness, stress, boredom and anger.
description=[*]Sometimes gives you something small: water, a snack, a lighter, cigarettes.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/gZKZgdk.png[/img]
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/kQIcnUu.gif[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Has her own inventory: clothes, bags, a worn axe to start with. Her things stay with her between sessions, after a death and after a crash.
description=[*]Wears almost anything you can, the way you do: clothing variants, left and right items both go on. Dress her straight from the floor, a container or off your own back.
description=[*]A bag she wears counts the way it does for you instead of filling up her inventory. She changes with her own hands.
description=[*]Washes up and does laundry at a sink, bath or barrel: her clothes and bags, blood on weapons, dirty bandages.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/wGPOl49.png[/img]
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/ue3mTC3.gif[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Wait here[/b]: she stays where you left her, even if you quit the game. She's on your world map and mini-map with her name.
description=[*]You can sleep next to her: she steps away while you sleep and comes back when you wake up.
description=[*]Time fast-forward works with her next to you.
description=[*]Rides with you: gets in and out at the door.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/pNBeuBy.png[/img]
description=
description=Right-click the house icon in the left sidebar, or right-click her.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Talk[/b]: when she's within 12 tiles. New options open up as you get closer.
description=[*][b]Treat my wounds[/b]: she patches you up right away.
description=[*][b]Wait here[/b] / [b]Come with me[/b].
description=[*][b]Fight[/b]: how they fight. [b]Stay[/b]: closer or farther.
description=[*][b]Work[/b]: clean up, or cut down trees.
description=[*][b]Flashlight[/b]: off, auto, or on while following.
description=[*][b]Other[/b]: take a rest, wash up and do laundry, say goodbye.
description=[*][b]Call[/b]: when she isn't with you.
description=[/list]
description=Left-click the house icon opens her window.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Info[/b]: her portrait with everything she wears, where she is and how close you are. Call her or say goodbye.
description=[*][b]Appearance[/b]: her or him. Body, skin, hair, beard, hair color and make-up. Save looks and switch between them.
description=[*][b]Settings[/b]: whether she treats your wounds on her own, whether she uses your medical supplies, whether hitting her hurts the relationship, whether she gives gifts on her own, and her name (default: Rin).
description=[/list]
